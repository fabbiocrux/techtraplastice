WP3 Multi-actor collaborations for just and safe plastic’s circular economy

Work Package 3: Multi-actor collaborations for just and safe plastic’s circular economy

This WP will encourage multi-actor initiatives to enhance the reuse of plastic involving waste pickers’ organizations, companies, and the local public sectors, taking advantage of the University’s articulation capacities.

Benchmark Study

The present benchmark will address the involvement of key players in the circular economy challenge that overpasses the margins of industries’ actions: the waste pickers’ organizations and the local public sectors.

The university has a privileged neutrality position that facilitates the attraction, orchestration and articulation of diverse actors in a shared scenario to exchange possibilities and generate win-win situations.

This WP will sensitize on the critical aspects of the “reuse” issues, learning from developments in Europe and other parts of the world.

The identified problems involving multi-actors visions and roles are foreseen to be problems that are also faced in other countries and cities. The benchmarking will help to find inspiration in how similar multilevel problems have been approached to start identifying clues of actions. The benchmarking study will be structured as a public document available for all the partners.

ECOPEK / CabelmaPET
Ecopek operated in the post-consumer stage of the PET value chain. Its business consisted of receiving recovered PET bottles, processing them, and transforming them into recycled raw material (rPET) suitable for reuse in industrial processes. In this regard, the initiative helped close the plastic packaging loop by transforming post-consumer waste into feedstock for the manufacture of new products, thereby reducing the need for virgin resin. The initiative was one of the leading examples of industrial PET recycling in Argentina, promoting the circular economy through the recovery of post-consumer materials.
