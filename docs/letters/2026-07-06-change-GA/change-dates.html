Dear Mr. Cabanne

This message is to present and justify several changes in the deadlines of the WP3 outcomes as follows:

  1. D3.1 Interviews reports: From 31 Oct 2026 to March 30 2027.
  2. D3.2 Benchmarking study: From 31 Oct 2026 to Nov 30 2026.
  3. D3.3 5 events reports (1 per country): From 31 Jan 2027 to 30 June 2027.
  4. D3.4 Updated portfolios”: From 31 Jan 2027 to 30 June 2027.
  5. MS3 Local stakeholders are engaged: From 31 Jul 2026 to 30 June 2027.
  6. MS4 External report on the reuse situation globally: From 31 Jul 2026 to 30 Nov 2026.

The main reason for these changes relates to the stakeholder involvement in the WP3 proves to be challenging and time consuming than expected. From the experience of Universidad Central that developed the activity in Jul 24/2026, the rest of HEIs in charge of the development of search conferences events (PUCV, UNC and UNRN) reflect upon the fact of needing an extension to ensure a comprehensive and well-documented engagement process. The implementation of the WP3 requires extensive coordination with external stakeholders representing the plastics value chain in each country. The preparation phase and scheduling the workshops depends on the availability of numerous socio-economic actors and therefore involves factors that cannot be fully controlled by the consortium. For this reason, the consortium proposes extending the WP3 to 31 August 2027.

Dear Mr. Cabanne,

Following our discussion on the last meeting, I am writing to present and justify the proposed changes to the deadlines of the WP3 deliverables and milestones, as detailed below:

  1. D3.1 – Interview Reports: from 31 October 2026 to 30 March 2027.
  2. D3.2 – Benchmarking Study: from 31 October 2026 to 30 November 2026.
  3. D3.3* – Reports on the 5 Events (1 per country)*: from 31 January 2027 to 30 June 2027.
  4. D3.4 – Updated Portfolios: from 31 January 2027 to 30 June 2027.
  5. MS3 – Local Stakeholders are Engaged: from 31 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.
  6. MS4 – External Report on the Reuse Situation Globally: from 31 July 2026 to 30 November 2026.

The main reason is that stakeholder engagement within WP3 has proven to be more challenging and time-consuming than initially anticipated. The implementation of WP3 requires coordination with external stakeholders representing the different actors across the plastics value chain in each participating country.

Based on the experience of Universidad Central, which carried out its activity on 24 July 2026, the other HEIs responsible for organising and implementing the Search Conference workshops (PUCV, UNC, and UNRN) have identified the need for additional time to ensure a comprehensive and well-documented stakeholder engagement process. The implementation period of WP3 until 31 August 2027 will ensure that all Search Conferences are successfully organised, their outcomes are properly documented, and the resulting action plans can effectively support the implementation of Work Package 4.

We remain committed to ensure broader stakeholder participation ensuring the quality and pertinence of all WP3 activities and outputs.

Thank you for your all support and consideration to TechTraPlastiCE project.

Best Regards,

Fabio Cruz

*PS: The title of this deliverable is the object of the amendment proposition.

Dear EU Commisison,

On behalf of the TechTraPlastiCE consortium, we kindly request Amendment AT21 to Grant Agreement No. 101179564 concerning the Description of the Action (DoA). We request the correction of the title of Deliverable D3.3 under WP3 – “Multi-actor Collaborations for a Just and Safe Plastic Circular Economy” as follows:

  • Current title: “5 Events Reports (1 per country)”
  • Proposed title: “Four Search Conference Reports”

As specified in Task 3.4 of the DoA, four search conferences workshops are planned at the following HEIs:

  1. Universidad Central (UC), Colombia
  2. Pontificia Universidad Católica de Valparaíso (PUCV), Chile
  3. Universidad Nacional de Córdoba (UNC), Argentina
  4. Universidad Nacional de Río Negro (UNRN), Argentina

The current title results from a typographical error, for which we apologise. The proposed correction removes this inconsistency and aligns the deliverable title with the activities already defined in the approved DoA.

This amendment does not change the nature, scope, or ambition of the project. The deliverable will document the preparation, implementation, and outcomes of the search conferences workshops. They are designed as strategic planning tools to bring together relevant actors and foster multi-actor collaboration. The experience of Universidad Central further confirms the relevance of this approach within the circular economy transition.

We kindly ask you to consider and approve this correction.

Thank you for your all support and consideration to TechTraPlastiCE project.
